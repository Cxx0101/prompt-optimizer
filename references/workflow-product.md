# Workflow: Product, E-commerce, and Advertising Images

## Start from communication goal

Identify whether the image is:

- clean catalog/e-commerce;
- hero advertisement;
- lifestyle placement;
- ingredient/material story;
- technical feature visualization;
- social media creative;
- packaging or launch key visual.

## Product fidelity is a hard constraint

Preserve:

- silhouette;
- proportions;
- color;
- material;
- logo/label placement;
- openings, buttons, seams, connectors, handles, caps;
- distinctive geometry.

Do not beautify by redesigning the product.

## Product photography controls

Specify:

- camera height/angle;
- background/set surface;
- key/fill/rim arrangement in plain visual terms;
- reflection hardness;
- shadow softness;
- material-specific highlights;
- negative space for copy;
- crop and aspect ratio.

## Advertising narrative

One hero message per image. Use props only if they reinforce scale, use case, ingredient, or brand world.

## Common failures

- warped packaging text;
- changed logo;
- impossible product geometry;
- floating product with no contact shadow;
- reflections inconsistent with the set;
- too many decorative props competing with the hero product.
